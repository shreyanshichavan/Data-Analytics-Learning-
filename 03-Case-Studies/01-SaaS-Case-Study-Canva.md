# Case Study 01: SaaS Business – Canva

## What is SaaS?

SaaS stands for Software as a Service.

It is a software delivery model where users use software through the internet instead of installing and purchasing the software permanently on their computer.

Users usually access the software through a website or application and may pay a monthly or yearly subscription.

### Examples of SaaS

- Canva
- Google Drive
- Zoom
- Microsoft 365
- Salesforce

---

## Case Study: Canva

Canva is an online design platform that allows users to create different types of digital content such as presentations, posters, resumes, social media posts, videos, and other designs.

As a SaaS company, Canva provides its services online and earns revenue mainly through subscriptions and other business offerings.

A Data Analyst can help Canva understand how users interact with the platform and identify areas where the business can improve.

---

## What Data Can Canva Collect?

A SaaS company like Canva can generate a large amount of data from its users and business activities.

Some examples include:

- Number of new users
- User registrations
- Login frequency
- Number of designs created
- Templates used by users
- Features used by users
- Time spent on the platform
- Subscription plans
- Free and paid users
- Pro subscription upgrades
- Subscription cancellations
- User location
- Customer feedback

---

## Role of a Data Analyst in Canva

A Data Analyst studies this data to understand user behaviour and business performance.

The analyst may answer questions such as:

### 1. How many users are signing up?

The analyst can track new registrations over time and identify whether the number of new users is increasing or decreasing.

This helps the company understand its user growth.

### 2. Which templates are used the most?

The analyst can identify the most popular templates and categories.

For example:

- Presentation templates
- Resume templates
- Instagram posts
- Posters
- Business templates

This information can help Canva improve its template library.

### 3. How often do users create designs?

The analyst can study how frequently users create designs.

If users are creating more designs, it may indicate higher engagement with the platform.

### 4. Which features are used the most?

Canva provides many features such as templates, text tools, image editing, AI tools, and collaboration features.

A Data Analyst can identify which features users use the most and which features are rarely used.

### 5. How many users upgrade to Canva Pro?

A Data Analyst can analyze the number of free users who become paid subscribers.

This helps the business understand its conversion rate and improve its subscription strategy.

### 6. Why are users cancelling their subscriptions?

The analyst can study cancelled subscriptions and look for possible reasons.

For example:

- High subscription cost
- Users not finding enough value
- Lack of required features
- Low platform usage
- Better alternatives

This analysis can help the company reduce customer churn.

---

## Important KPIs for a SaaS Data Analyst

Some important Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) include:

- User Growth
- Daily Active Users (DAU)
- Monthly Active Users (MAU)
- Customer Conversion Rate
- Subscription Conversion Rate
- Customer Churn Rate
- Customer Retention Rate
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- User Engagement

---

## Example Business Question

Suppose Canva notices that many users create an account but do not purchase a Pro subscription.

The Data Analyst can investigate:

1. How many users register every month?
2. How many users remain free users?
3. How many users start a trial?
4. How many users convert to Pro?
5. At which stage are users leaving?
6. Which features are used by Pro users?
7. What factors are associated with subscription cancellation?

After analyzing the data, the analyst can provide insights to the business team.

---

## Possible Insight

Suppose the analysis shows that:

- Many users create accounts.
- Most users use free templates.
- Users who use premium features are more likely to upgrade.
- A large number of users cancel after the trial period.

This may indicate that users are interested in premium features but are not finding enough value to continue their subscription.

The business can then investigate pricing, features, user experience, and subscription offers.

---

## How Data Analytics Helps the SaaS Business

Data Analytics helps a SaaS company to:

- Understand user behaviour
- Improve customer experience
- Increase user engagement
- Improve subscription conversion
- Reduce customer churn
- Identify popular features
- Improve products and services
- Support marketing decisions
- Increase revenue

---

## Conclusion

In a SaaS company, a Data Analyst plays an important role in understanding how users interact with the product.

The analyst converts user and business data into meaningful insights that help the company improve its product, retain customers, increase subscriptions, and make better business decisions.

The main idea is:

Raw Data → Analysis → Insights → Business Decision → Improvement
